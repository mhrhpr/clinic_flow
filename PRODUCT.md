# ClinicFlow Revenue OS — Product Definition

## Product thesis
ClinicFlow Revenue OS is not a generic clinic-management system. Its wedge is **revenue leakage detection and recovery** for Iranian aesthetic clinics.

The product answers four questions:
1. Where is money currently at risk?
2. Why is it at risk?
3. What should the team do next?
4. Did that action create a measurable business result?

## North-star loop
DATA → UNDERSTAND → DETECT → PRIORITIZE → RECOMMEND → EXECUTE → MEASURE → LEARN

## Initial ICP hypothesis
Owner-led aesthetic/skin/laser clinics with meaningful lead volume, high-ticket or repeatable treatments, 2+ operational staff, and acquisition through Instagram/WhatsApp/phone/web.

This is a hypothesis, not a validated market fact.

## Wedge
**Lost lead / follow-up recovery.**

Initial opportunity classes:
- new lead with no effective first follow-up
- overdue follow-up
- consultation without booked treatment
- appointment no-show
- completed treatment with expected next session but no booking
- inactive repeat-treatment candidate
- high-value lead with stalled progression

## Economic model
For every opportunity, ClinicFlow should expose:
- estimated value
- evidence
- urgency
- recoverability
- deterministic score
- confidence
- recommended next action
- action outcome

The first scoring model is deliberately deterministic. AI may explain, summarize, or suggest wording; it must not become the source of truth.

## MVP success metric
**Recovered or influenced revenue attributable to a ClinicFlow workflow**, measured with explicit baseline, action, outcome, and attribution notes.

No causal revenue claim is made from correlation alone.

## What we are deliberately not building first
- full accounting suite
- inventory management
- patient-facing marketplace
- autonomous messaging
- generic chatbot
- complex multi-agent orchestration
- dozens of dashboards

Those remain deferred until customer evidence supports them.

## Customer discovery questions
1. Walk me through a new Instagram/WhatsApp lead from first message to payment.
2. Where is the lead recorded?
3. Who owns the next follow-up?
4. How do you know a follow-up is overdue?
5. Show me the last 10 leads that did not convert.
6. Which of them could realistically have converted?
7. What is the typical ticket for those services?
8. How often do patients miss appointments?
9. How are missed appointments recovered?
10. How do you know which completed treatments are due for another session?
11. What report do you wish you had every morning?
12. What software do you already pay for?
13. What do staff still track in WhatsApp, Excel or memory?
14. What would make you pay for a system that recovers revenue?
15. What evidence would you require before trusting its recommendations?

## Validation gate
Before production automation, interview at least 10 relevant clinic operators and obtain structured evidence from at least 3 real workflows. A paid pilot or explicit willingness-to-pay signal is stronger evidence than feature requests.
